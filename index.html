01 / ABOUT

Payments is where technology, trust and human behaviour meet.

I’m Amandeep Batra, a payments strategy and performance leader focused on improving how digital commerce works at scale.

Today I lead Payments Performance Strategy across EMEA at Stripe, working with enterprise and strategic merchants, product teams and payment networks on the problems that sit between authorisation, authentication, regulation and conversion.

My work spans 3DS and authentication, payment optimisation, scheme and regulatory change, EMVCo, product strategy and the emerging world of agentic commerce.

Before Stripe, I built experience across financial services and payments, including time at Mastercard. I enjoy turning difficult industry questions into practical product and commercial decisions — and sharing what I learn.
