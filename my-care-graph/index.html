TRANSPARENT BY DESIGN

Provisional rules.
Not a clinical assessment.

This prototype is not validated and is not a diagnosis, risk prediction, or treatment recommendation. Thresholds, weights and freshness windows are illustrative and require clinical review.

Each indicator is linearly mapped between demo endpoints and clipped to 0–100. One indicator per correlated group is selected in the priority order below. Groups are averaged within a domain, then available domains are weighted. Contributions sum to the overall score.

Wearable measurements retain their device identity. Cross-device pairs are excluded from the six-month change; no equivalence is assumed. Change uses only the same selected indicators at both dates, with the current weights applied to both. New evidence is shown as coverage, not automatically as improvement.

Indicator endpoints & freshness

First available indicator in each group wins. HbA1c precedes glucose; sleep duration precedes efficiency; steps precede active minutes. Excluded correlated measurements stay visible. Sleep scoring here is deliberately simplified and must not be interpreted clinically.
